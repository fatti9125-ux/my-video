## 01 Washington Marching Army (34 s, 8 cuts)
- 0-1.7: close-up of Washington behind a tree of white feathers (hiding gag) in a log-cabin interior; the cut lands on a sullen farmer close-up (whiskey-rebel), purple shadow over his face, angry brows, held. At 2.7, a whiskey jug with hose slides into frame; caption "WHISKEY REBELLION" (white caps, black outline, lower third), held to the cut.
- 4.25-8.6: extreme close-up of Washington (face fills the frame) against a flag + bushes. Smug: brows down-in, half smile. At 6.1 the eagle mascot (black suit) pops in at bottom-left, small. At 6.4 (frame 10 of the strip, one-frame swap) Washington's face swaps to raised arched brows, flat mouth, sweat drop: an instant reaction swap with no in-between. Per-frame motion during the hold is about 1.5 (continuous slow camera drift), never 0.
- 8.75-11: wide grass field; Washington on a white horse, militia crowd on the right. Name card "GEORGE WASHINGTON": the whole frame darkens about 70% for about 1.75 s (9.5-11.2), text centred, white Montserrat ExtraBold with a soft shadow, no outline. Hard cut back to normal brightness, same shot.
- 11-15.25: caption "13,000 MILITIA" lower-centre over the same shot. The horse walks in place while the camera slowly pulls back and pans. The militia (duplicates of one rig with muskets) bob.
- 15.25-20: camera pulls way back (zoom-out over about 1 s) into a tiny army on a painted mountain/forest landscape, then hold. The landscape is painted (soft, textured), muted greens.
- 20.25-23.5: tracking shot. Horse + Washington + marching militia move right-to-left relative to a scrolling painted background (dirt road, mountains); the camera follows. Horse legs cycle and soldiers bob on 1s. A dotted-outline "?" keyhole silhouette (an unknown, unseen figure) walks with them.
- 23.75-30: top-down-ish wide shot of columns of militia (grid of duplicated sprites) marching; Washington rides beside them; he fades/dissolves out at about 28-29 (opacity fade, about 1 s).
- 30-31.5: close-up of Washington smug again, then a quick camera move (frames 6-10: about 5-frame eased push/pan) re-frames him to the right side.
- STAMP at frame 11 (31.55 s): "THE LAST PRESIDENT TO LEAD AN ARMY PERSONALLY", red ink, rectangular border, rotated about -8°, NO white box, translucent ink with grunge. Frame 11 is huge (text overflows the frame); frames 12-15 shrink; frame 16 has settled top-left = **5-frame slam** (eased, decelerating). The background is NOT darkened here (light scene). About 4 frames after the stamp lands, Washington swaps to worried brows + sweat drop (a reaction to the stamp). The stamp holds about 2 s to the end.
- SFX (from the audio pass): see the audio section.
