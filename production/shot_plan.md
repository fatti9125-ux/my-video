# Pilot shot plan: Roosevelt → Coolidge (VO part 3, lines 4–16)

- **Composition:** `VPPilot` (`src/VP/Pilot.tsx`; place plates in `src/VP/PilotBg.tsx`). 1920×1080 at 30 fps, rendered at 4K (scale 2) to `out/VP_Pilot_Roosevelt_to_Coolidge_4K.mp4`.
- **Length:** 56.4 s of VO (part-3 time 21.14–77.50 s) plus a 1 s tail hold. 1,721 frames.
- **VO:** the narrator's master file `public/tg2/vo/vp_full.mp3`. Part 3 starts at 220.02 s, so the pilot plays master time 241.16–297.52 s. Word timings come from `src/VP/ep/vp3.json` (the forced alignment already used by the film). The timing is therefore the real VO, not provisional.
- **Spec:** every choice below cites `reference_analysis/Animation_Production_Spec.md` (§ numbers) and the clip:frame evidence it rests on.

Unless noted otherwise, the following rules apply to every shot:
- **Expression swaps:** 1 frame, landing on the motivating word, with listeners following 4 f later (§2.2).
- **Camera:** one continuous move per shot (§6).
- **Captions:** hard on and off, white heavy caps, bottom-centre (§0 rule 7).
- **Name cards:** the shot's own plate dimmed, with centred caps and no box (§0 rule 6). Faces stay clear of the card text (standing rule).
- **Characters:** whole sheet drawings or `Act` face-swap bodies, so heads are attached by construction (skill rule 34).

## Asset provenance

- **Characters:**
  - The user's own character sheets (`public/tg2/vp/*`); expressions are baked by `face_swap.py`.
  - Quill is the original Thanksgiving Quill (`public/tg2/Poses/quill`).
- **Plates:** all new vector paintings written for the pilot (`src/VP/PilotBg.tsx`). Layouts follow the real places in the public-domain or free references gathered in `scratchpad/bgref/INDEX.md` (Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress, NPS), which were used for reference only and not composited in.
- **VO:** the user's narrator master. Quiet SFX come from the project library (`public/tg2/sfx`). No music.
- **Facts checked:**
  - TR took the oath on 14 Sept 1901 at the Ansley Wilcox House, Buffalo, aged 42, the youngest president.
  - Platt pushed him to the VP slot in 1900.
  - Coolidge was sworn in on 3 Aug 1923 at 2:47 a.m. by his father, a notary, by kerosene lamp at Plymouth Notch. He is the only president sworn in by his father.
  - Harding died on 2 Aug 1923 in San Francisco.
  - The "August 2, 1923" caption marks the night the news arrived. The oath itself was after midnight on the 3rd, which is why "2:47 A.M." is shown.
